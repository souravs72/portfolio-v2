Four years of backends

I'm Sourav Singh, a backend developer in Kolkata. I spent four years on Java and Spring Boot at TCS and an investment company before ERP work took over, and I have built on Frappe and ERPNext since 2024. Along the way I built a workflow system used by 7,000+ people, ERPNext integrations with WhatsApp, Postman and OpenAPI, and a CI/CD pipeline that cut deploys from two hours to thirty minutes. I work the way an ERP needs: every change in a custom app under Git, staging before production, backups restored rather than assumed, and updates written for the people who run the business, not for other developers. Questions

Who can upgrade my ERPNext from v14 or v15 to v16? I do it as a fixed-price project: a Health Check first, then the upgrade on a staging copy, fixes for anything that breaks, your team's sign-off, and only then production.

Do you edit ERPNext or Frappe core? Never. Every change lives in a separate custom app under Git, so the next update can't wipe it out.

Can you take over a system someone else built? That's most of the work. The Health Check maps what was changed, where, and what it will cost to make safe.

Do you work with clients outside India? Yes. I keep overlap hours with the US, UK, Europe, the Gulf and Africa, and invoice international clients in USD.

Self-hosted or Frappe Cloud? Both: benches on your own server or in Docker, and Frappe Cloud sites.

How long does an upgrade take? One version hop usually takes one to three weeks, including the days your team spends testing. The Health Check gives you a date.

Who owns the code? You do. It lives in your repository from the first day, with notes on every change.

What does ongoing support cost? Care plans start at $500 a month for a fixed block of hours, with patches and monitoring included.

Do you build new features too? Yes: DocTypes, workflows, reports, print formats, portals and integrations, built as proper Frappe apps with tests.
